# D - Domain norms for HPO annotation UIs vs Phentrieve

Scope: read-only benchmark. Sources are web (fetched/searched 2026-10-27) plus the
Phentrieve frontend source on `main` (e05b646). No screenshot was taken; findings
are from source. Line refs are relative to `frontend/src/`.

## 1. Conventions and expectations

| # | Convention | Who does it | Source |
|---|-----------|-------------|--------|
| C1 | **Matched span highlighted in the note, negation shown on the span itself** (Doc2Hpo: light-green span, separate red "N" modifier you can click to flip). Reviewers check negation where they read the text, not in a side list. | Doc2Hpo | [Doc2Hpo NAR 2019 (PMC6602487)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6602487/), [live tool](https://doc2hpo.wglab.org/) |
| C2 | **Human-in-the-loop correction is cheap**: select text to add, double-click to delete, click the concept to swap. Doc2Hpo found that this hybrid workflow beat both pure manual curation (P 0.86) and pure automatic extraction (P 0.39), reaching P 0.92, and cut curation time from about 7 to 5 min per 1000 words. | Doc2Hpo | [PMC6602487](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6602487/) |
| C3 | **Present vs absent is a first-class binary, recorded explicitly** (PhenoTips stores observed and not-observed features; Phenopacket `excluded=true` means "looked for and found absent"; LIRICAL lists "observed" and "excluded" terms separately and weights excluded terms heavily). | PhenoTips, GA4GH Phenopackets, LIRICAL | [Phenopacket PhenotypicFeature](https://phenopacket-schema.readthedocs.io/en/latest/phenotype.html), [LIRICAL phenotype score](https://lirical.readthedocs.io/en/stable/phenotype-score.html), [PhenoTips figure (PMC5467641)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5467641/figure/F1) |
| C4 | **Term label is primary, ID is secondary but always visible and copyable.** Ontology browsers show label, accession, definition, and synonyms together, so users can disambiguate near-synonyms. | HPO browser, OLS4, Monarch | [Monarch 2024 NAR (PMC10767791)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10767791), [OLS4](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/) |
| C5 | **Pick the most specific term.** Phenomizer guidance tells users to choose the most specific term, because broad terms weaken the match. That only works if the definition and the hierarchy context are visible at selection time. | Phenomizer | [Kohler et al. AJHG 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2009.09.003), [GenomeMed 2016 review](https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1186/s13073-016-0261-8) |
| C6 | **Output is a portable HPO ID list.** Clinicians paste lists into Exomiser, LIRICAL, Phen2Gene, PubCaseFinder, or a lab requisition. Doc2Hpo copies a Phenolyzer-ready list straight to the clipboard. ClinPhen outputs a ranked list "attachable to a summary note". | Doc2Hpo, ClinPhen, Exomiser | [PMC6602487](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6602487/), [ClinPhen (Genet Med 2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41436-018-0381-1), [Exomiser analysis docs](https://exomiser.readthedocs.io/en/13.1.0/advanced_analysis.html) |
| C7 | **Phenopacket is the interchange format, and negation must survive export** (`excluded`). Real curated phenopackets average 2.7 negated terms per case. | GA4GH, LIRICAL | [Phenopacket schema](https://phenopacket-schema.readthedocs.io/en/latest/phenotype.html), [LIRICAL phenopacket input](https://lirical.readthedocs.io/en/latest/phenopacket-hpo.html), [PMC7477017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7477017/table/tbl1) |
| C8 | **Confidence needs interpretation, not just a number.** High displayed confidence increases trust and over-reliance and lowers accuracy. Clinicians ask for explanations that help them *interpret* confidence. Aim for appropriate trust, not maximal trust. | CDS / XAI studies | [arXiv 2501.16693](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16693), [Bussone et al. 2015](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/13150), [JMIR Hum Factors 2025](https://humanfactors.jmir.org/2025//e79658/) |
| C9 | **Set expectations up front and show why.** "Make clear what the system can do", "make clear how well it can do it", "make clear why it did what it did" (G1, G2, G11). Hedged wording ("possible match") leaves room for error. | Microsoft HAI guidelines | [Amershi et al. guidelines](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-design/) |
| C10 | **Keyboard-first, fast entry for power users.** PhenoTips has a quick-search box with error-tolerant, predictive search. Multi-line clinical text needs Enter to insert a newline, with submit on Ctrl/Cmd+Enter. | PhenoTips, common web convention | [SciCrunch PhenoTips record](https://scicrunch.org/resolver/SCR_006340) |
| C11 | **Privacy messaging sits at the point of entry and is specific**: what is sent, to whom, and whether it is stored. Do not bury it in settings. | HAI G1/G2; GA4GH practice | [Amershi et al.](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction-design/) |
| C12 | **Disclaimers are short, one-time, and non-hostile.** Over-explaining increases cognitive load and stress, and that kind of friction drives dismissal without reading. | XAI / CDS studies | [arXiv 2501.16693](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.16693) |

Caveat: the searches did not reach the hpo.jax.org term page (it returned 404), PhenoTips docs, or Face2Gene UI documentation. C4 and C10 rely on secondary descriptions and general knowledge of those tools.

## 2. Gap analysis vs Phentrieve

What already matches well: the HPO ID links out to the browser (`components/PhenotypeCardRow.vue:18-28`). Note spans are keyboard-operable and have a focus ring (`components/query/FullTextWorkspace.vue:43-63`). Manual edits get a distinct violet hue (`FullTextWorkspace.vue:222-229`). The curator supports change-term, remove, and undo (`components/FullTextNoteCurator.vue:43-55`), which covers C2. A PII review dialog exists (`locales/en.json:105-114`). Phenopacket export exists (`composables/usePhenotypeCollection.js:113+`). A synonyms and definition drawer exists (`components/ResultItem.vue:88-118`).

| Conv. | Gap | Evidence |
|------|-----|----------|
| C1 | **Negation is not shown on the note spans.** The segment builder drops `status`, and every span renders in the same blue whether the finding is affirmed, negated, or uncertain. A clinician reading "no seizures" sees it highlighted exactly like "seizures". | `composables/useUserNoteAnnotations.js:302-308` (range has no status), `:339-347` (segment has no status); `components/query/FullTextWorkspace.vue:43-52` (only `--manual`/`--active` classes); `:208-229` |
| C1/C3 | **The negation colour changes between views.** Results show orange `warning`, the collection shows pink, and note spans show nothing. The red `error` colour is reused for *low similarity*, so red means two different things. | `components/ResultItem.vue:145-155`, `components/PhenotypeCollectionPanel.vue:82-84`, `components/SimilarityScore.vue:160-166` |
| C3 | **The present/absent toggle is ambiguous.** The icon shows the *target* state (a check icon appears on a negated row), and the tooltip just says "Toggle Assertion Status". Uncertain gets no colour of its own. | `components/PhenotypeCollectionPanel.vue:104-122`, `locales/en.json` `assertionToggle` |
| C4 | **ID is visually primary and the label secondary.** The ID is bold on the first line and the label sits below it in body-2. The collection panel does the same (`<strong>` ID, label as subtitle). | `components/PhenotypeCardRow.vue:26,32-34`; `components/PhenotypeCollectionPanel.vue:78,91-93` |
| C5 | **The definition is hidden behind an info icon**, so users cannot tell near-synonym candidates apart at a glance. A rank badge (1, 2, 3...) takes up the prepend slot instead. | `components/ResultItem.vue:7-9, 21-48, 88-98` |
| C6 | **There is no copy-to-clipboard for the HPO list.** "Export as Text" downloads a `.txt` file that has a header, a timestamp, and a TSV. Unused i18n keys `copyButton`/`copySuccess` ("Copy HPO Terms") suggest the feature was planned. | `composables/usePhenotypeCollection.js:101-111`; `locales/en.json` `queryInterface.copyButton` (no `.vue` references) |
| C7 | The text export writes `negated`, while Phenopacket uses `excluded` and LIRICAL says "excluded". There is no "observed / excluded" grouping. This is minor. | `composables/usePhenotypeCollection.js:106-108` |
| C8/C9 | **The raw cosine score is shown as the headline number (0.00-1.00).** It is labelled "Excellent / High / Moderate / Low / Poor Match", and the tooltip scale runs from "No Match" to "Perfect Match". Cosine is not calibrated, and "Excellent"/"Perfect" invite over-reliance, which is the risk C8 describes. In the default ranking, an "Excellent" result at 0.90 says nothing about clinical correctness. | `components/SimilarityScore.vue:3-12, 37-79, 126-138`; thresholds `constants/defaults.js:28-31`; `locales/en.json` `resultsDisplay.quality.*`, `perfectMatch` |
| C10 | **Pressing Enter in the multi-line clinical-note textarea submits the form**, so typing a note with line breaks is impossible. There is no visible shortcut hint. | `components/query/QueryForm.vue:36` (`@keydown.enter.prevent` on `v-textarea`) |
| C11 | Privacy and LLM-processing disclosure appears only inside Advanced Options, and only when the LLM backend is selected. Next to the textarea there is nothing about what happens to pasted text. | `components/AdvancedOptionsPanel.vue:286-293`; `components/query/QueryForm.vue:20-41` |
| C9/i18n | **Copy and naming are inconsistent.** The welcome text and disclaimer say "research text", but the textarea says "Clinical note". The drawer is titled "Case Workspace" through hard-coded per-locale labels, while en.json calls it "HPO Collection" and the FAB tooltip comes from the same hard-coded map. Several user-facing strings bypass i18n even though the app is multilingual: "Clinical note", "Phenotype query", "Paste or type a clinical note", "Linked phenotype", "Edit annotation:", "Negated"/"Uncertain", "View {id} in HPO Browser". | `components/query/QueryForm.vue:33,39,59`; `components/PhenotypeCollectionPanel.vue:291-322`; `components/query/FullTextWorkspace.vue:57,68`; `components/ResultItem.vue:147,151`; `components/PhenotypeCardRow.vue:23`; `locales/en.json` `queryInterface.welcomeText`, `disclaimerDialog.description` |
| C12 | The disclaimer is a persistent modal with a heading, a description, 4 limitations, a responsibility paragraph, and "I Understand and Agree". This is acceptable and shown only once, but it is long. One item uses `v-html`. Low priority. | `components/DisclaimerDialog.vue:2-66`, `:31` |

## 3. Ranked improvements (clarify what exists; no new features)

1. **Show negation on note spans** (C1). Carry `ann.status` into ranges and segments, add an `annotated-note-span--negated` class (for example a strike-through or dashed underline plus a muted colour), and put "Excluded" in the span tooltip. Small change: `useUserNoteAnnotations.js:302-347`, `FullTextWorkspace.vue:47-51,208+`. This has the highest impact because missing it silently reverses meaning.
2. **Fix Enter in the note textarea** (C10). Enter inserts a newline and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter submits. Add a tiny hint ("Ctrl+Enter to map"). This is a one-line change at `QueryForm.vue:36` plus a hint string.
3. **Use one vocabulary and one colour for present/absent/uncertain everywhere** (C1, C3, C7). Use "Present" / "Excluded" / "Uncertain" (Phenopacket wording) with one colour each, and stop using red for low similarity. Replace the inverse-icon toggle with a labelled two-state chip or button group ("Present | Excluded"). Files: `ResultItem.vue:145-155`, `PhenotypeCollectionPanel.vue:79-122`, `SimilarityScore.vue:160-166`.
4. **Reword the score so it doesn't read as diagnostic confidence** (C8, C9). Make the qualitative band primary and hedged ("Close match / Possible / Weak"). Keep the number secondary. In the tooltip, replace "Perfect Match" and "Excellent" with "Text similarity to the term (0-1), not a probability of being correct". This only touches copy and labels in `SimilarityScore.vue` and `en.json` `resultsDisplay.quality.*`.
5. **Make the label primary and show a one-line definition inline** (C4, C5). Swap the order in `PhenotypeCardRow.vue:17-34` so the label comes first and the ID becomes a small monospace secondary line. Show a single truncated definition line under the label in `ResultItem.vue`, and keep the expand control for synonyms. Consider dropping the rank badge, since the order already shows rank.
6. **Add "Copy HPO IDs" to the collection panel** (C6). Copy `HP:xxxx label` lines, excluded terms prefixed or grouped as "Excluded:", with a snackbar confirmation. The i18n keys `copyButton`/`copySuccess` already exist. Also add a small copy icon on each ID in `PhenotypeCardRow`.
7. **Add a one-line privacy note under the note textarea** (C11). For example: "Text is sent to the Phentrieve server for processing; saved history stores findings, not the note text. Remove direct identifiers." Reuse the existing redaction behaviour as fact. If LLM mode is on, show the existing `llmDisclosure` there and not only inside Advanced Options.
8. **Unify naming and finish i18n** (C9). Pick one name for the drawer (for example "Phenotype list") and move `CASE_WORKSPACE_BRIDGE_LABELS` into the locale files. Align "research text" with "clinical note". Move the hard-coded strings listed in the gap table into `en.json`, and run `make frontend-i18n-check`.
9. **Shorten the disclaimer** (C12). Use 2-3 bullets and keep the full text behind a "Details" expander. Leave the one-time acknowledgement as it is.

Skipped as feature creep: hierarchy/parent-term display, "suggest more specific term", gene prioritisation links (Doc2Hpo-style), and onset/severity modifiers.
